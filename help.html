Seattle Food & Bev help

The list comes from your Google Sheet. The app only reads your restaurant tab; everything it saves goes to the "app data" tab.

Reading a card

🚌 17 minTransit time from where you are (or your planned start). The walk is listed too when it's under 25 minutes.
1.2 miStraight-line distance, for places beyond the nearest 15 or when there's no signal.
🕒 / ⚠️ / 🔴Open until a time and how long you'd have; under an hour is flagged; closed when you'd arrive.
🍹 / 🏷Happy hour on (or starting within 30 min) when you'd arrive, or a special that day. These places move to the top.
💳 inKindThe deals or description cell says "inkind". Also moves to the top when open.
⭐ 💚 ✓From your "would recommend" section; a green-highlighted row; marked visited in the app.

Tap a card to open the place in Google Maps.

Planning ahead

  • Tap 📍 Near me · now to pick a starting place, a time, or both.
  • Pick on map, or press and hold the map, to start from a dropped pin.
  • Back to here and now resets it.

Setting up another phone

  • At the bottom of the list, tap Share setup link and send the link privately. Opening it once connects that phone; then add the page to the home screen.
  • The link works like a password to your list. If it ever gets out, see "The link got out" below.
  • With no signal the list still opens. For a map with no signal, download the area in the Google Maps app (profile picture → Offline maps); tapping a card opens it there.

Changing the list

  • Add, edit, or remove places in the restaurant tab of the Sheet, then tap Refresh. New places get their location and hours overnight, or right away from the Sheet's Seattle Food & Bev → Fill missing info menu.
  • Deals are read from the deals column: days and times like HH m-f 4-6, daily 5-6, 9-close, or $2 oysters mondays. "Happy hour" with no times is shown but can't be ranked.
  • Type inkind in a place's deals cell to give it the inKind chip.
  • A green fill on the name marks a favorite. Rows under your avoid heading never appear.

The "app data" tab

  • The script manages it. Two things are yours to type in the Status column: ok (the Google match is right, stop flagging it) and skip (not a single place, so no location).
  • Red rows weren't found on Google; yellow rows matched a different name. Fixing the name or neighborhood in the restaurant tab makes it look again.
  • Visits you mark in the app are saved here: date, verdict, deals seen, notes.

Costs and limits

  • The Sheet's Seattle Food & Bev → Show Google usage this month menu shows how much of each free allowance is used. The script stops itself at about 90% of each.
  • Travel times are reused if you've moved less than 200 m in the last 10 minutes.
  • The map view's own limit is the daily cap set on the map key in Google Cloud.

If something's wrong

  • "Couldn't update the list": no signal. The app shows the copy it saved last time, sorted by straight-line distance.
  • List is A–Z: location is blocked or off. Allow it for this site in Chrome's site settings.
  • "The key doesn't match": use Change Sheet connection at the bottom of the list and paste the URL and key again.
  • The link got out: in Apps Script, run resetKey, then showAppSetup, and enter the new key on each phone.
